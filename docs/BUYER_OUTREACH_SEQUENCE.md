# Buyer Outreach Sequence — Day 0 / 5 / 14 / 30

Status: **Draft sequence. Nothing sent, no recipient assigned.** A
4-touch outreach cadence for each of the three buyer types this project
already has ready introduction content for
(`docs/BUYER_OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md`). Day 0 in each version below is
that existing template, referenced rather than duplicated; Days 5, 14,
and 30 are new. Every email keeps the same discipline as the originals:
no supplier names, no capacity/MOQ/lead-time figures beyond "reviewed
per enquiry," no certification claim beyond Ghana FDA and the confirmed
GEPA registration.

## Cadence logic

| Day | Purpose | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Introduction — who BorgaFoods is, why reaching out, an open invitation to talk | Professional, direct, low-pressure |
| 5 | Follow-up — acknowledges they're likely busy, restates the core ask briefly | Brief, no new information |
| 14 | Value-based follow-up — adds something genuinely new (not just a reminder) so it doesn't read as nagging | Informational, gives a reason to reconsider |
| 30 | Final follow-up — closes the active sequence, leaves the door open without further scheduled contact | Respectful, no pressure, clearly the last scheduled touch |

If a buyer responds at any point, the sequence stops immediately and
the conversation continues individually — none of these are meant to
send automatically once a reply exists
(`docs/BUYER_RESEARCH_DATABASE_TEMPLATE.md`'s "Responded" status is the
signal to stop).

## What every follow-up deliberately avoids

- **No new unsupported claims introduced at Day 14** — the "value" added
  is real (GEPA credential, the exporter profile document, more detail
  on the review process), not an invented differentiator.
- **No escalating pressure tactics** — no artificial urgency, no
  discount/incentive offers (none exist to offer), no "last chance"
  framing beyond honestly noting Day 30 is the last *scheduled* touch.
- **No product substitution in Sequence C** — every private-label
  follow-up stays scoped to Fufu Flour, matching
  `docs/BUYER_TARGETING_FRAMEWORK.md`'s Tier 2 scope note.

## What's needed before this sequence can actually run

1. A decision on tooling — nothing in this codebase sends email on a

4. Business sign-off to begin outbound outreach at all — this document
   prepares the content, it doesn't authorize sending it.

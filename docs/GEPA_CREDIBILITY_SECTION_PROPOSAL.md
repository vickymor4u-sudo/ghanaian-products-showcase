# GEPA / Export Credibility Section — Proposal

Status: **Draft only. Not published on the website or any company
profile.** Contains an open question that must be resolved before any
version of this section is published — see below.

## The open question, first

The certificate supplied shows:

- Registered entity: **SUPPLY AND DEMAND WORLDWIDE LIMITED**
- Registration No.: **GEPA2018800113**
- Dated issue: **26 February 2018**
- **Date of Expiry: 26 February 2019**
- Printed footer: *"This Certificate is Subject to Annual Renewal!"*

Taken at face value, this document proves Supply and Demand Worldwide
Limited **was** a GEPA-registered commercial exporter as of 2018, on a
certificate that itself expired in 2019 pending annual renewal. It does
not, on its own, establish that the registration is current in 2026 —
seven years and (per the certificate's own terms) roughly seven renewal
cycles later. A public web search for the registration number and
company name did not surface an independent, current confirmation
either (GEPA does not appear to publish a public real-time registration
lookup).

**Before any wording below is published anywhere** — the website, a
LinkedIn page, an association application, a buyer-facing profile —
one of the following is needed:

- A current or renewed certificate (any date after Feb 2019), **or**
- Direct confirmation from GEPA that registration No. GEPA2018800113 is
  presently active, **or**
- Confirmation from BorgaFoods that the annual renewal has in fact been
  kept current since 2018 and the 2018 certificate shown is simply the
  most recent copy on hand (common — the original certificate is often
  kept even after renewal, if GEPA reissues a renewal notice/sticker
  rather than a whole new certificate; this repository doesn't know
  which applies here).

Everything below is written to be safe to finalize the moment one of
those three is confirmed — it is not blocked on rewriting, only on that
confirmation.

## Proposed section — Option A (if registration is confirmed current)

For `/about`, positioned near the existing "Key Facts" panel:

> **Registered Exporter**
> Supply and Demand Worldwide Limited is registered with the Ghana
> Export Promotion Authority (GEPA) as a commercial exporter
> (Registration No. GEPA2018800113).

For the company profile / LinkedIn About section, one sentence:

> Registered commercial exporter with the Ghana Export Promotion
> Authority (GEPA), Registration No. GEPA2018800113.

## Proposed section — Option B (conservative, historical-fact-only phrasing)

Usable even if renewal status can't be immediately confirmed, since it
makes no claim about *current* standing — only that registration
happened, which the certificate does prove:

> Supply and Demand Worldwide Limited first registered as a commercial
> exporter with the Ghana Export Promotion Authority (GEPA) in 2018.

**Caution even here**: if the registration has actually lapsed and was
never renewed, even this historical phrasing could read as implying
present-day standing to a buyer who doesn't parse it that carefully.
Recommended only as a fallback if Option A's confirmation genuinely
can't be obtained quickly — Option A (or simply waiting until renewal
status is confirmed) is the better outcome.

## Why this matters enough to build the section now, pending confirmation

The Growth Monitoring cycle (`docs/GROWTH_TRACKING_LOG.md`) already
found GEPA's exporter directory surfacing independently in competitor
searches — real external corroboration that buyers and search engines
already route through GEPA's system when evaluating Ghanaian exporters.
A confirmed, current registration number is a genuine, verifiable trust
signal exactly of the kind `docs/EXTERNAL_AUTHORITY_TRUST_FRAMEWORK.md`
set out to find — worth having ready to publish the moment it's
confirmed, rather than starting the drafting process from scratch then.

## What this repository will do once confirmed

Apply whichever of Option A/B is appropriate (or a business-supplied
alternative) to `/about`, following the same draft → review → apply
pattern as every prior claim change this project has made
(`docs/PUBLIC_CLAIM_VERIFICATION_AUDIT.md` is the precedent), plus add
the confirmed line to `docs/LINKEDIN_COMPANY_PAGE_PACKAGE.md`'s About
section and `docs/EXPORTER_PROFILE_ONE_PAGER.md`.

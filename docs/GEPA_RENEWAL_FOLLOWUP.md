# GEPA Renewal Follow-Up — Superseded by Confirmation

Status: **Superseded, 9 August 2026 — BorgaFoods confirmed the renewal
directly, so the outreach drafted below was not needed and was not

## The question this was originally prepared to resolve

Registration No. GEPA2018800113 (Supply and Demand Worldwide Limited)
was issued 26 Feb 2018 with a printed Date of Expiry of 26 Feb 2019 and
"Subject to Annual Renewal" on the certificate. Whether it had been kept
current since then was unknown to this repository at the time. **This is
now resolved** — BorgaFoods confirmed directly that the renewal is
completed and the registration is current. See
`docs/GEPA_CREDIBILITY_SECTION_PROPOSAL.md`'s confirmation record for
exactly what is and isn't established by that confirmation (no new
expiry date or renewed certificate is on file).

The draft email and document checklist below are preserved unedited, in
case they're useful for a future annual renewal or a request for a
written GEPA confirmation letter — not because they're still an open
action item today.

## What GEPA's own site confirms, and what it doesn't

A search of GEPA's public Exporters Portal (gepaghana.org) confirms
exporters can register **or renew** their export certificate through
that portal, that registration is renewable annually, and that
first-time registration requires the GEPA Registration Form plus
photocopies of registered business certificates — with additional
commodity-specific documents (e.g. Quality Assurance certificates,
phytosanitary certificates) depending on the product category. It does
**not** publish a specific, step-by-step renewal procedure distinct
from first-time registration, and does not publish a fee schedule. That
gap is reported honestly here rather than filled with an invented
process, consistent with how this project has handled every other
association/registration process it couldn't fully verify (see
`docs/EXTERNAL_AUTHORITY_TRUST_FRAMEWORK.md` §3 for the same pattern
with the 4 trade associations).

**Practical implication**: the fastest, most reliable path to an answer
is direct contact with GEPA — by phone (published contact:
+233-302-740909) or through the Exporters Portal itself — rather than
guessing at a renewal-specific process this repository can't confirm.
